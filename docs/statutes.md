# LeaseAudit: Statutory Grounding & Legal Audit Trace

This document provides a comprehensive, authoritative legal audit trace for every statutory rule codified in LeaseAudit. Every rule in LeaseAudit is grounded in official, primary legal authorities (United States Code, state statutory codes, or municipal housing codes).

---

## 1. Federal Baseline (United States)

Official Source: [United States Code (U.S.C.) - Legal Information Institute / Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text)

### 1.1 Fair Housing Act - Familial Status Discrimination
- **Statutory Citation:** `42 U.S.C. § 3604(a)` (incorporating 42 U.S.C. § 3602(k))
- **Rule ID:** `FED-FHA-FAM-01`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Unlawful to refuse to sell, rent, or negotiate, or make unavailable or deny a dwelling to any person because of race, color, religion, sex, familial status, or national origin.
- **Legal Context:** Landlords cannot implement "adults only" rules, categorical bans on children, or per-child surcharges/rent increases unless the facility qualifies as certified "Housing for Older Persons" under 42 U.S.C. § 3607(b).
- **Official URL:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/3604

### 1.2 Fair Housing Act - Assistance & Service Animals
- **Statutory Citation:** `42 U.S.C. § 3604(f)(3)(B)` (HUD / DOJ Joint Guidance on Reasonable Accommodations)
- **Rule ID:** `FED-FHA-DIS-02`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Discrimination includes a refusal to make reasonable accommodations in rules, policies, practices, or services, when such accommodations may be necessary to afford a disabled person equal opportunity to use and enjoy a dwelling.
- **Legal Context:** Assistance animals (trained service animals and emotional support animals) are not "pets" under federal law. Landlords cannot charge pet deposits, pet fees, or "pet rent" for verified assistance animals or enforce blanket "no pets" policies against them.
- **Official URL:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/3604

### 1.3 Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA) - Early Lease Termination
- **Statutory Citation:** `50 U.S.C. § 3955`
- **Rule ID:** `FED-SCRA-MIL-03`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** A lessee on active military duty may terminate a residential lease without penalty upon delivery of written notice and a copy of military orders for deployment (not less than 90 days) or a Permanent Change of Station (PCS).
- **Legal Context:** Any lease clause waiving SCRA early termination rights or assessing early termination fees/liquidated damages against deploying servicemembers is void under federal law.
- **Official URL:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/50/3955

### 1.4 Residential Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction Act of 1992
- **Statutory Citation:** `42 U.S.C. § 4852d` (24 C.F.R. Part 35 / 40 C.F.R. Part 745)
- **Rule ID:** `FED-LEAD-DISC-04`
- **Severity:** `Watch`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Before a purchaser or lessee is obligated under any contract to purchase or lease target housing (constructed prior to 1978), the seller or lessor must provide an EPA-approved lead hazard information pamphlet and disclose known lead-based paint hazards.
- **Legal Context:** Requires affirmative disclosure and pamphlet delivery; clauses acknowledging lead without supplying disclosures require tenant verification.
- **Official URL:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/4852d

## 2. California

Official Source: [California Legislative Information (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes.xhtml)

### 2.1 Security Deposit Maximum Cap (AB 12)
- **Statutory Citation:** `Cal. Civ. Code § 1950.5(c)`
- **Rule ID:** `CA-SEC-DEP-CAP-01`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Legislative History:** Amended by Assembly Bill 12 (Haney, 2023), effective July 1, 2024.
- **Statutory Text Summary:** A landlord may not demand or receive security, however denominated, in an amount or value in excess of an amount equal to one month's rent.
- **Statutory Exception:** Narrow exception for a "small landlord" (a natural person or LLC composed entirely of natural persons owning no more than 2 residential rental properties comprising no more than 4 total dwelling units), who may demand up to two months' rent, unless the tenant is a service member.
- **Official URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1950.5.&lawCode=CIV

### 2.2 Security Deposit Return Timeline & Deductions
- **Statutory Citation:** `Cal. Civ. Code § 1950.5(g)`
- **Rule ID:** `CA-SEC-DEP-RET-02`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** No later than 21 calendar days after the tenant has vacated the premises, the landlord shall furnish the tenant with an itemized statement of deductions and refund the remaining balance. Bad faith claim or retention subjects landlord to statutory damages of up to twice the amount of security under § 1950.5(l).
- **Official URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1950.5.&lawCode=CIV

### 2.3 Landlord Notice of Entry
- **Statutory Citation:** `Cal. Civ. Code § 1954`
- **Rule ID:** `CA-ENTRY-NOT-03`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** A landlord may enter only under statutory conditions (emergency, agreed repairs, showings, abandonment) and must give the tenant reasonable written notice (presumed to be 24 hours) during normal business hours.
- **Legal Context:** Blanket clauses permitting entry "at any time without notice" or waiving notice of entry are void under California Civil Code § 1953(a)(1).
- **Official URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1954.&lawCode=CIV

### 2.4 Prohibition of Self-Help Evictions & Lockouts
- **Statutory Citation:** `Cal. Civ. Code § 789.3`
- **Rule ID:** `CA-EVICT-SLF-04`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** A landlord shall not with intent to terminate occupancy prevent the tenant from reasonable access by changing the locks, removing outside doors or windows, removing tenant personal property, or terminating utilities.
- **Remedy:** Actual damages plus statutory penalties of up to $100 for each day of violation (minimum $250) and reasonable attorney's fees.
- **Official URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=789.3.&lawCode=CIV

### 2.5 Implied Warranty of Habitability (Non-Waivable)
- **Statutory Citation:** `Cal. Civ. Code § 1941.1 & § 1942.1`
- **Rule ID:** `CA-HABIT-REP-05`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlords must maintain dwellings in a tenantable condition (effective waterproofing, operational plumbing, gas/electric, heat, clean grounds). Under § 1942.1, any agreement by a lessee waiving or modifying rights under § 1941 is void as against public policy.
- **Official URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1941.1.&lawCode=CIV

### 2.6 Late Fees & Liquidated Damages Standard
- **Statutory Citation:** `Cal. Civ. Code § 1671(d)`
- **Rule ID:** `CA-LATE-FEE-06`
- **Severity:** `Watch`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** In residential leases, liquidated damages provisions are void unless the party seeking enforcement establishes that it would be impracticable or extremely difficult to fix actual damage. Late fees must represent a reasonable endeavor to estimate actual administrative loss (*Garrett v. Coast & Southern Fed. Sav. & Loan Ass'n*, 9 Cal.3d 731).
- **Official URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1671.&lawCode=CIV

## 3. New York

Official Source: [New York State Senate Legislation (nysenate.gov)](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws)

### 3.1 Security Deposit One-Month Cap
- **Statutory Citation:** `N.Y. Gen. Oblig. Law § 7-108(1-a)(a)`
- **Rule ID:** `NY-SEC-DEP-CAP-01`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Legislative History:** Enacted under the Housing Stability and Tenant Protection Act of 2019 (HSTPA).
- **Statutory Text Summary:** No deposit or advance shall exceed the amount of one month's rent under such contract. Demands for last month's rent in addition to a security deposit are prohibited.
- **Official URL:** https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108

### 3.2 Security Deposit 14-Day Return & Deduction Forfeiture
- **Statutory Citation:** `N.Y. Gen. Oblig. Law § 7-108(1-a)(e)`
- **Rule ID:** `NY-SEC-DEP-RET-02`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Within fourteen days after the tenant has vacated the premises, the landlord shall provide an itemized statement indicating the basis for deductions and return remaining funds. If the landlord fails to provide the statement and deposit within fourteen days, the landlord forfeits any right to retain any portion of the deposit.
- **Official URL:** https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108

### 3.3 Landlord Reasonable Notice of Entry & Quiet Enjoyment
- **Statutory Citation:** `N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 235-b / NYC Admin. Code § 27-2008 / Quiet Enjoyment`
- **Rule ID:** `NY-ENTRY-NOT-06`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory & Regulatory Basis:** Statewide common law implied covenant of quiet enjoyment; NYC Housing Maintenance Code § 27-2008 (mandatory 24 hours advance written notice for inspections, 1 week for repairs); NY Attorney General Tenants' Rights Guide.
- **Official URL:** https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-rights

### 3.4 Statutory Late Fee Cap ($50 or 5%) & 5-Day Grace Period
- **Statutory Citation:** `N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 238-a(2)`
- **Rule ID:** `NY-LATE-FEE-03`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** No late fee may be charged unless rent is late by at least five days, and the fee cannot exceed fifty dollars or five percent of the monthly rent, whichever is less.
- **Official URL:** https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/238-A

### 3.5 Unlawful Eviction Criminalization (Self-Help Prohibition)
- **Statutory Citation:** `N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 768`
- **Rule ID:** `NY-EVICT-SLF-04`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** It is a Class A misdemeanor for any person to unlawfully evict or attempt to evict an occupant by force, threat, changing locks, padlock installation, or discontinuation of essential services (heat, water, electric). Civil penalties range from $1,000 to $10,000 per violation.
- **Official URL:** https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/768

### 3.6 Statutory Warranty of Habitability (Non-Waivable)
- **Statutory Citation:** `N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 235-b`
- **Rule ID:** `NY-HABIT-WAR-05`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Implied in every residential lease is a covenant that the premises are fit for human habitation. Any agreement waiving or modifying this warranty is void as contrary to public policy.
- **Official URL:** https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-B

### 3.7 Roommate Law / Unlawful Restrictions on Occupancy
- **Statutory Citation:** `N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 235-f`
- **Rule ID:** `NY-OCCUP-ROOM-07`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Any lease provision restricting occupancy solely to the named tenant or prohibiting roommates is void as against public policy. Tenants have the statutory right to share the dwelling with immediate family and one additional occupant plus dependent children.
- **Official URL:** https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/235-F

## 4. Texas

Official Source: [Texas Constitution and Statutes (statutes.capitol.texas.gov)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/)

### 4.1 Security Deposit 30-Day Return & Accounting
- **Statutory Citation:** `Tex. Prop. Code § 92.103 & § 92.104`
- **Rule ID:** `TX-SEC-DEP-RET-01`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** The landlord shall refund a security deposit to the tenant on or before the 30th day after the tenant surrenders the premises, conditioned on tenant providing a written forwarding address. Landlord may not retain any portion for normal wear and tear and must provide an itemized list of deductions.
- **Official URL:** https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.92.htm#92.103

### 4.2 Late Fee Mandatory 2-Day Grace Period & Statutory Caps
- **Statutory Citation:** `Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019`
- **Rule ID:** `TX-LATE-FEE-02`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlord may not charge a late fee unless rent has remained unpaid for at least two full days after the due date. Total late fees cannot exceed 12% of monthly rent (4 units or fewer) or 10% (5+ units), unless higher actual damages are proven. Overcharging incurs statutory penalties of $100 plus 3x the fee charged.
- **Official URL:** https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.92.htm#92.019

### 4.3 Prohibition of Unlawful Lockouts & Tenant Exclusion
- **Statutory Citation:** `Tex. Prop. Code § 92.0081`
- **Rule ID:** `TX-LOCKOUT-SLF-03`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlord may not intentionally prevent a tenant from entering the leased premises except by judicial eviction process (with extremely narrow, hyper-strict key replacement procedures). Lockouts without 24-hour key availability incur civil penalties of 1 month's rent plus $1,000.
- **Official URL:** https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.92.htm#92.0081

### 4.4 Prohibition of Utility Interruption
- **Statutory Citation:** `Tex. Prop. Code § 92.008`
- **Rule ID:** `TX-UTIL-SHUT-04`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlord or landlord's agent may not interrupt or cause the interruption of utility service paid directly to a utility company, or submetered electrical or water services, to force payment of delinquent rent.
- **Official URL:** https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.92.htm#92.008

### 4.5 Landlord Duty to Repair / Non-Waivable Habitability
- **Statutory Citation:** `Tex. Prop. Code § 92.052, § 92.056 & § 92.006`
- **Rule ID:** `TX-HABIT-REP-05`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlords have a statutory duty to make a diligent effort to repair conditions materially affecting the physical health or safety of an ordinary tenant. Under § 92.006(c), this duty cannot be waived by lease contract.
- **Official URL:** https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.92.htm#92.056

## 5. Florida

Official Source: [Online Sunshine (leg.state.fl.us)](http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/)

### 5.1 Security Deposit Return (15-Day / 30-Day Claim)
- **Statutory Citation:** `Fla. Stat. § 83.49(3)(a)`
- **Rule ID:** `FL-SEC-DEP-RET-01`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlord has 15 days to return the deposit if no damage claim is made. If imposing a claim for damages, landlord has 30 days to give written notice by certified mail. Landlord forfeits the right to impose a claim if notice is not timely sent.
- **Official URL:** http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0000-0099/0083/Sections/0083.49.html

### 5.2 Landlord Notice of Entry for Repairs (24 Hours - Miya's Law)
- **Statutory Citation:** `Fla. Stat. § 83.53(2)`
- **Rule ID:** `FL-ENTRY-NOT-02`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Legislative History:** Amended by Senate Bill 898 ("Miya's Law"), increasing notice of entry for repairs from 12 hours to 24 hours.
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Reasonable notice for the purpose of repair is notice given at least 24 hours prior to the entry, between 7:30 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. Entry without notice is permitted only in genuine emergencies.
- **Official URL:** http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0000-0099/0083/Sections/0083.53.html

### 5.3 Prohibited Practices (Self-Help Eviction & Utility Shutoff)
- **Statutory Citation:** `Fla. Stat. § 83.67`
- **Rule ID:** `FL-PROHIB-PRAC-03`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlord shall not cause the termination or interruption of any utility service, change door locks, or remove outside doors or tenant belongings. Violations incur statutory damages of actual damages or 3 months' rent, whichever is greater, plus court costs and attorney's fees.
- **Official URL:** http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0000-0099/0083/Sections/0083.67.html

### 5.4 Landlord Duty to Maintain Premises (Non-Waivable Habitability)
- **Statutory Citation:** `Fla. Stat. § 83.51 & § 83.47`
- **Rule ID:** `FL-HABIT-REP-04`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Landlords must comply with applicable building, housing, and health codes and maintain roofs, windows, doors, floors, and plumbing in reasonable repair. Under § 83.47, lease provisions waiving these requirements are void and unenforceable.
- **Official URL:** http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0000-0099/0083/Sections/0083.51.html

## 6. Illinois

Official Source: [Illinois General Assembly (ilga.gov)](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs.asp)

### 6.1 Security Deposit Return Act (30-Day Itemization / 45-Day Return)
- **Statutory Citation:** `765 ILCS 710/1`
- **Rule ID:** `IL-SEC-DEP-RET-01`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Scope:** Applies to residential properties containing 5 or more units.
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Lessors withholding any deposit portion for damage must deliver an itemized statement within 30 days of surrender and return the remaining deposit within 45 days. Failure to deliver itemized statements forfeits deduction rights.
- **Official URL:** https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=076507100K1

### 6.2 Forcible Entry and Detainer Act (Self-Help Prohibition)
- **Statutory Citation:** `735 ILCS 5/9-101 et seq.`
- **Rule ID:** `IL-EVICT-SLF-02`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** No person shall make an entry into lands or tenements except where entry is allowed by law, and in such cases, only peaceably. Self-help evictions, changing locks, and physical repossessions outside of court orders are prohibited.
- **Official URL:** https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=073500050K9-101

### 6.3 Retaliatory Eviction Ban & Code Violation Reporting
- **Statutory Citation:** `765 ILCS 720/1`
- **Rule ID:** `IL-RETALIAT-03`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Declared against public policy for a landlord to terminate or refuse to renew a lease because a tenant has complained to any governmental authority regarding code or ordinance violations.
- **Official URL:** https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=076507200K1

### 6.4 Security Deposit Interest Act (25+ Units)
- **Statutory Citation:** `765 ILCS 715/1`
- **Rule ID:** `IL-DEP-INT-04`
- **Severity:** `LikelyUnenforceable`
- **Statutory Scope:** Applies to residential real property containing 25 or more units.
- **Statutory Text Summary:** Lessors holding security deposits for more than 6 months shall pay interest annually to the lessee on the deposit at the rate established by the State Treasurer. Lease waivers are void.
- **Official URL:** https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=076507150K1
